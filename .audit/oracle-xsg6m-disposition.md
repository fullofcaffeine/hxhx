7. Hard-cut the three selected planners to index views across ordinary, nested, standalone, and focused fixture paths.
8. Compare the parent and candidate in clean environments with the pinned Haxe compiler.
9. Expand only when the measured first slice reduces CPU without output, diagnostic, identity, or memory drift.
10. Stop expansion when another measured owner dominates or complete regeneration has sufficient margin.

The design stays small because the index owns structure only. It does not schedule planners, cache across requests, or expose a generic Reflaxe API.
